Project Highlights

  • Analyzed delivery delays, customer retention, restaurant ratings, and order trends.
  • Built SQL queries for revenue analysis, delivery SLA, and customer cohorts.
  • Created Power BI dashboards for operational insights and business decision-making.
  • Performed end-to-end data cleaning and exploratory data analysis using Python.

Key Insights

  • Weekend orders were 34% higher than weekdays.
  • Mumbai showed the highest late delivery rate at 11.2%.
  • Top 20% of customers generated 61% of total orders.
  • Orders beyond 5km were significantly more likely to be delayed.
